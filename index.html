Delivery, Refund & Privacy Policies

Clear terms governing digital software licenses issued by G.K Innovation.

1. Digital Delivery & Activation

RecoilX is a purely digital Android software utility. No physical goods are shipped. Upon successful payment via our authorized payment processors (Paystack or Flutterwave), Pro access is unlocked automatically on the buyer's registered account within seconds.

2. Refund & Billing Policy

All new devices receive a 3-day full-feature free trial prior to purchase so users can verify compatibility with their Android device. Because digital Pro access is activated immediately upon confirmation, completed purchases are non-refundable except in verified cases of accidental duplicate billing or unactivated technical payment errors reported within 48 hours of the transaction. Plans are one-time term passes (30, 90, or 365 days) and do not silently auto-charge without user consent.

3. Privacy & Data Protection

G.K Innovation collects only the account email address and an anonymized Android device identifier strictly to authenticate user sessions, enforce single-device licensing, and prevent trial abuse. Payment card and bank details are processed exclusively by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and are never stored on our servers. We never sell or rent user data to third parties.

4. Software Disclaimer

RecoilX is an independent screen calibration and visual overlay utility developed by G.K Innovation. It does not modify system memory, intercept network traffic, or alter third-party application files.
